308 /** Take ownership of an op the kernel may still complete.
309
310 For member-owned ops (e.g. a multishot accept arming) whose
311 owner goes away on a *non-terminal* path — the owning acceptor
312 adopting a different descriptor, say. Draining the op's CQEs

314 dispatching, which is only tolerable while the whole context
315 is being torn down.

318 reserved, until the kernel delivers its terminal CQE. That is
